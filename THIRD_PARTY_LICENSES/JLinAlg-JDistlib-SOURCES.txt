QQuadraticFormDistribution.java adapts JLinAlg's positive-mixture implementation.
Its source header retains upstream attribution. Local changes cache coefficients,
extend the term budget, and fail early for unresolvable spectra. The upstream
namespace remains unmodified; this adapter is in gov.nih.eqtl.settest.

The release SHA-256 pins and exact incorporated namespaces are in pom.xml.
Native-provider service registrations and vendor runtimes from the upstream
all-jar are not incorporated. GPU eQTL's existing backend dependencies and
their separate license notices remain applicable.
